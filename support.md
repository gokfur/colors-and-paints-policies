# Support: Colors and Paints

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to **[gokfurca@icloud.com](mailto:gokfurca@icloud.com)**.

**Where are my child's pictures?** Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. A parent can add a finished picture to Photos (the app only adds, it never reads your photos) or share it, after the parental question.

**Does it need internet?** No, it works fully offline.

**What does it cost? Does it have ads or purchases?** It is a one-time paid app: you buy it once on the App Store. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no tracking.

**How do I change the language?** On the home screen tap the gear, then Language, and choose one of the ten languages: Turkish, English, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Portuguese, Japanese, Russian, Arabic or Urdu. The app changes at once.

**I hear no sound.** Check the side switch on your iPhone (ring or silent): the app's sounds follow it. Music and sound effects can also be turned off or on in Settings.

**What is the parental question?** A question for a grown-up: type a three-digit number that is written in words. It always appears before saving, sharing or resetting everything, and a parent can also put Settings behind it (Settings > Parental Gate, off by default).

**Something is cut off, slow or wrong.** Please write to us with your device, iOS version and the language, and what you saw.
