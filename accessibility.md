# Accessibility: Colors and Paints

**Last updated: 9 November 2026**

What the app does today:
- **VoiceOver labels** on buttons, pictures and progress.
- **Reduce Motion:** animations are simplified when the iOS setting is on.
- **Large touch targets:** small areas of a picture have bigger invisible touch areas, and buttons are large.
- **Sound and haptics** can each be turned off in Settings.
- **Phone and tablet, portrait and landscape.**

What we are still improving: full Dynamic Type support and a color-blind friendly palette mode. If something does not work for you, please write to us and we will fix it: **[gokfurca@icloud.com](mailto:gokfurca@icloud.com)**
