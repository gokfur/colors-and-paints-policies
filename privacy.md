# Privacy Policy: Colors and Paints

**Last updated: 21 November 2026**

Hi, we are Gokfur6ix, the small team behind Colors and Paints. The honest answer is short: **we collect nothing.**

## What the app does with data
- **Nothing leaves the device.** The app has no account, no sign-in and never connects to the internet. We have no servers.
- **Paintings and progress** (the colors a child picked, stars, and settings such as the language, sound on or off) are saved **only on the device**. Deleting the app deletes them. They may be part of the device owner's own iCloud or iPhone backup, which Apple controls.
- **No ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking.** The app contains no third-party code (no SDKs), no ad networks and no tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier, so iOS does not show a tracking question.
- **Saving and sharing pictures (parents only).** A parent can save a finished picture to the device's Photos or share it with the system share sheet, after passing the parental gate. The app only adds the picture to Photos; it never reads the photo library, and nothing is sent to us. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.
- **No links out.** The app has no links to websites or social networks. Saving, sharing and resetting always ask a parental question first, and a parent can also put Settings behind that question.

## Children
The app is made for children and is designed for the Apple Kids category. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to ask a parent to consent to (COPPA in the United States, PIPEDA in Canada, GDPR-K in the EU and UK, KVKK in Türkiye).

## Pictures and sounds
Pictures, music and sound effects are created for the app. The app is not affiliated with any brand, film, cartoon, game or character.

## Changes
If a future version adds online features or in-app purchases, this page will be updated in the same release, before it ships, and the change will be described in the App Store release notes.
