# Privacy Policy: Colors and Paints

**Last updated: 9 November 2026**

Hi, we are Gokfur6ix, the small team behind Colors and Paints. The honest answer is short: **we collect nothing.**

## What the app does with data
- **Nothing leaves the device.** The app has no account, no sign-in and never connects to the internet. We have no servers.
- **Paintings and progress** (the colors a child picked, stars, and settings such as sound on or off) are saved **only on the device**. Deleting the app deletes them. They may be part of the device owner's own iCloud or iPhone backup, which Apple controls.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The app contains no third-party code (no SDKs), no ad networks and no tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier, so iOS does not show a tracking question.
- **No permissions.** The app does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
- **No links out.** The app has no links to websites or social networks. Settings is behind a parental gate.

## Children
The app is made for children and is designed for the Apple Kids category. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to ask a parent to consent to (COPPA in the United States, PIPEDA in Canada, GDPR-K in the EU and UK, KVKK in Türkiye).

## Pictures and sounds
Pictures are made with the help of artificial intelligence and by our team. Music and sound effects are created for the app. The app is not affiliated with any brand, film, cartoon, game or character.

## Changes
If a future version adds online features or purchases, this page will be updated in the same release, before it ships, and the change will be described in the App Store release notes.
